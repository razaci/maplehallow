Pawhaven Clinic

You run the only clinic in fictional Maple Hollow. Real problems walk in: blocked cats, bloat scares, owners who cannot pay, and a surgery table that never quite stays empty.

Play is card-based. Move patients between rooms. Choices spend energy, cash, and supplies. Some choices open Amazon product pages so the tools feel like real clinic gear — this is a game, not medical advice.

Not a substitute for real veterinary care. Amazon links are optional shopping searches. Handle euthanasia and money conversations as story choices only.
